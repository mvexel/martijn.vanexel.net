This weekend, I rode a [S24O](https://www.bikeovernights.org) to Sun Tunnels with some of my bikepacking buddies. In the process, we rode a stretch of the [abandoned original grade](http://www.abandonedrails.com/first-transcontinental-railroad) of the Transcontinental Railroad, which is celebrating its [150th anniversary](https://spike150.org) next month!
