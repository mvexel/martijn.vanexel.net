I [wrote about](https://mvexel.prose.sh/20241013-what-the-quad) [What The Quad](https://quad.osm.lol), a little tool I wrote to quickly get relevant, copyable information about a map tile, in a previous post. I added a few features today:

- Bounding Box Mode: you can now draw a bounding box and get its coordinates in the standard, comma-separated format. 
- Copy as WKT / GeoJSON: you can now copy the bounding box, in both the quadkey mode and the new bbox mode, as GeoJSON and WKT strings.

That's all! Let me know if you find this useful.
